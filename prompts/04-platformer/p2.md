CAMERA
Follows the player center with a centered deadzone (camera.deadzoneW x deadzoneH); outside it, moves toward the player by min(1, camera.lerp*dt) of the gap. Clamped to the level bounds; on an axis where the view is larger than the level, the level is centered. Snaps to the player on respawn and level start.

LEVELS
Replace the test grid with levels.list, played in order. Each level is rows (strings, row 0 at the top) of tileSize tiles: levels.solidSymbol solid, "." empty, levels.playerSymbol start, other symbols = entity with that symbol. Validate on load: pad short rows with "." and warn in debug; no start -> first empty tile of the bottom row; no goal -> warn in debug. Pre-render all solid tiles once per level to an offscreen canvas with levels.tileLook (surface fill, line outline); split in chunks if larger than 4096 px.
Enemies and coins come from pools sized by limits; an enemy that falls below the kill zone is despawned silently.

RUN STRUCTURE
- Lives (rules.lives) are kept across levels; score too.
- Level time counts from level start (frozen in pause).
- Goal touched: timeBonus = max(0, timeBonusMax - levelTime*timeBonusPerSecond), add to score, levelClear sound, go to LEVEL_CLEAR (a state like RESULT without sendScore) showing time and bonus with a "Next level" button (confirm). After the last level's goal -> RESULT win.
- Lives 0 -> RESULT lose.
- Coin: +points, collect sound, "+N" popup. Checkpoint: ring fills with special color, checkpoint sound. Stomp: stomp sound. Landing faster than 300 u/s: land sound + squash.

SCORE AND SAVE
Score = coins + stomps + time bonuses. On RESULT: sendScore(score), save best and progress {bestLevel}.

EDGE CASES
- Respawn while an enemy stands on the checkpoint: invulnerability prevents instant death.
- Pause while a touch button is held: released, must be pressed again after resume.
- Landing exactly on a tile corner slides off (X resolved first), never snags.
- maxJumps 1 disables the double jump cleanly; 3 allows a triple jump.

Check: complete both levels with keyboard, then with the on-screen buttons; die in the pit and on spikes (respawn at checkpoint); lose all lives; no errors.
