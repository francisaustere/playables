GAME: PLATFORMER (skeleton template, primitives only). Run, jump, double-jump up a tile level to the goal, stomp patrollers, avoid spikes and the pit. Put in game_spec.json (next prompts add more):
{{GAME_SPEC:meta,display,input,rules,player,entities}}

PLAYER (hitbox = look w x h at bottom-center, enemies too)
dir = right - left (both = 0). vx moves toward dir*runSpeed at accel (ground, dir != 0), decel (ground, dir 0) or airAccel (air). vy += gravity*dt, capped at maxFall. jumpV = sqrt(2*gravity*jumpHeight), doubleJumpV = sqrt(2*gravity*doubleJumpHeight).
A jump press is buffered jumpBuffer s. Leaving ground without jumping starts coyoteTime. With a buffered press: grounded or coyote -> vy = -jumpV, jumpsUsed = 1; else if jumpsUsed < maxJumps -> vy = -doubleJumpV, jumpsUsed++. When coyote expires after walking off, jumpsUsed = 1. Releasing jump while vy < 0: vy *= jumpCut once. Landing resets jumpsUsed.

TILES
Level = array of row strings, "#" solid, "." empty, other symbols = entities by symbol, "P" start. Axis-separated AABB vs nearby solid tiles: move X, resolve (vx = 0); move Y, resolve (landing = grounded, ceiling = vy 0). For now: tileSize 64 and a small test grid of your own with every symbol, fitting the screen (levels section next prompt).

ENEMIES (kind enemy)
Walk at speed, gravity, collide with tiles, turn at walls and, if turnAtEdges, when the tile under the leading foot is empty. Stomp: overlap, player vy > 0 and player bottom last step <= enemy top + stompTolerance -> enemy dies, +points, vy = -stompBounce, jumpsUsed = 1. Otherwise hurt (unless invulnerable): lose a life, knockback away by hurtKnockback, invulnerable rules.invulnerableTime . Stomp beats hurt in the same step.

HAZARDS AND GOALS
Spikes (kind hazardTile, hitbox = bottom half of the tile) and the kill zone (player top > rows*tileSize + rules.killZoneOffset): lose a life, respawn at the last checkpoint with no velocity, invulnerable. Checkpoint: first touch sets the respawn point. Goal: ends the level (next prompt). Lives 0 -> RESULT lose.

ON-SCREEN BUTTONS (gesture "button:name")
Squares of touchButtons.size, alpha, left and right side by side bottom-left (gap), jump bottom-right, anchored to screen corners + safe margin. Pointer down holds the action, sliding left<->right switches, multi-touch and mouse work. If showOnlyAfterTouch: visible after a touch input or on coarse-pointer devices, hidden after keyboard input.

ORDER per step: input, player, tile collision, enemy, contacts, kill zone, camera (next prompt), end. Skins read only look. Check with keys and buttons.
