ENDINGS
An ending replaces the event card with an ending card: title, text, avatar of the last character, accent color positive for outcome "win" and hazard for "lose". Tap, click or Enter continues to RESULT. Save progress {endings: [unique ids found]}; best = most days; sendScore(days).

HUD (anchored to visible screen edges + safe margin)
- Top-left: text.day + turn ("Day 12"). Top-right: pause.
- Stat row under the HUD: icons, bars (track line, fill in stat color), small labels. A stat within 15 % of an extreme pulses.
- Bottom: the two choice buttons with the current labels.

SCREENS
- Title: game title, best days, text.endingsFound "3/10", control hints text.controlsKeyboard / text.controlsTouch (highlight the last input type used), text.tapToStart; an idle card swaying gently.
- Result: text.win or text.lose, ending title and text, days, best with "New best!", text.newEnding when this ending is found for the first time, endings found X/10, buttons Play again and Menu.

FEEDBACK AND SOUND (style.feedback, audio.sfx)
- Card arrives: slide in + cardIn. Lean: tilt, highlights, preview dots + lean sound. Choose: card flies off + action sound.
- Stat up/down: bar tween, icon flash positive/hazard, statUp/statDown; with previewMode "full" also a "+N/-N" popup.
- Ending card: ending sound; Result: win or lose sound.
Cache each card's wrapped text and drawing in an offscreen canvas.
