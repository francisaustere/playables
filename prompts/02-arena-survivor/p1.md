GAME: ARENA SURVIVOR (skeleton template, primitives only). Top-down arena larger than the screen: move in 8 directions, shoot chasing enemies, survive. Put in game_spec.json (next prompts add more):
{{GAME_SPEC:meta,display,input,rules,world,player,weapon,entities}}

WORLD AND CAMERA
Origin top-left, border walls of wallThickness, solid rects world.obstacles (x,y top-left) drawn with obstacleLook. Player starts at the center. Camera += (player - camera) * min(1, cameraLerp*dt), clamped so the view stays inside the world (centered if the view is larger).

INPUT
move and aim are vectors; key groups {up,down,left,right} give a normalized 8-direction vector. joystickLeft / joystickRight = floating joystick for pointers that START in that screen half; a pointer keeps its role until released (two thumbs work). mouseAimRight = mouse button held, started in the right half: aim = normalize(pointer world pos - player).

PLAYER
velocity moves toward move*speed by at most accel*dt. Colliders are circles of radius look.size/2. Pushed out of walls/obstacles (circle vs AABB), slides. Enemy contact: hp -= touchDamage, invulnerable rules.invulnerableTime (blink), knockback away from the enemy.

WEAPON (rules.fireMode)
Cooldown fireInterval. directional, or both with |aim| > input.aimDeadzone: fire along aim. auto, or both without aim: fire at the nearest enemy within autoRange, none = no shot. Projectiles: projectileSpeed, projectileLife, damage, pass through pierce enemies, die on walls/obstacles.

ENEMIES (entities with kind "enemy")
Steer toward the player at speed, collide with walls/obstacles. Hit = white flash; hp 0 = particles + destroy sound. For now spawn a random type every 1 s on the border of the view + world.spawnMargin, inside the walls, never in view.

ORDER per step: input, player, weapon, spawn, enemy, projectile, collision, pickup (next prompt), camera, end. hp <= 0 -> RESULT lose; rules.duration > 0 reached -> RESULT win.

Skins read only look; everything spawned comes from pools. Check: move with keys, mouse, touch; aim with IJKL, mouse, right thumb while moving; no errors.
