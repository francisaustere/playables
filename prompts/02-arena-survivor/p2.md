PICKUPS (entities with kind xp, heal, magnet)
- Killed enemy drops one gem worth its xp, then rolls heart dropChance, then magnet dropChance for an extra item.
- Gem: inside player.magnetRadius it flies to the player at magnetSpeed (ramping up from 0 over magnetRamp s); collected inside pickupRadius: xp += value, collect sound. If the gem pool is full, add the value to the oldest gem instead of spawning.
- Heart: hp = min(maxHp, hp + heal), heal sound, not magnetized. Magnet: every gem on the map becomes magnetized until collected, powerup sound. Items despawn after their life (s) and blink during the last rules.itemBlinkTime s.
- At run start, place world.scatterGems gems (value 1) at random free spots so exploring pays.

SPAWNER
- Every curve(difficulty.spawnInterval, t) s spawn one enemy; type chosen by weighted random with weights curve(difficulty.typeWeights[id], t) (0 = never). Position on the border of the visible rect expanded by world.spawnMargin, clamped inside the walls, retried 4 times if inside an obstacle, else skipped. Never inside the view; if the view covers the whole world, spawn on the world edge farthest from the player.
- From t = waveInterval, every waveInterval s: a wave of curve(waveSize, t) chasers evenly placed on a circle around the player (radius = half the visible diagonal + spawnMargin), wave sound.
- Enemy speed is multiplied by curve(difficulty.enemySpeedScale, t).
curve() = linear interpolation between {at, value} points, holding the last value.

CROWD AND COLLISIONS
Rebuild a uniform grid (limits.collisionCell) each step. Use it for projectiles vs enemies, enemies vs player, nearest-enemy search and separation: overlapping enemies push apart by difficulty.separation * overlap (same and neighbour cells only). Several enemies touching the player in one step = one damage instance. A projectile hitting two enemies in one step damages the nearest first, then applies pierce.

SCORE, END, SAVE
Score = xp collected (sendScore value). Track kills and time. Lose at hp 0; win when rules.duration > 0 is reached; duration 0 = endless (curves hold after their last point). On RESULT: sendScore(xp), save best and progress {bestTime, totalKills}.

ROBUSTNESS
Pools sized by limits (enemies, projectiles, gems, items, particles); empty pool = skip spawn, never allocate. Gems outside the view + magnet radius are not updated unless globally magnetized. Pause releases joysticks; on resume they must be pressed again. Resize recomputes the camera clamp and spawn ring.

Check with debug.invincible and spawnInterval forced to 0.1 for 3 minutes: pools saturate without errors and FPS stays at least 30.
